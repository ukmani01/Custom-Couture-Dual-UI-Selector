About Vitalis

Built as a hospital, run like a promise kept.

Vitalis Hospitals opened in Bandra West with one goal: make specialist care coordinated instead of scattered.

Our story

Vitalis began as a 120-bed cardiac and orthopaedic centre. Over a decade, it grew into a 300-bed multi-speciality hospital — adding oncology, neurology, nephrology and women's health, while keeping every department on one shared medical record.

That's still the idea today: a patient referred from cardiology to nephrology shouldn't have to repeat their history, redo a scan, or wait a week for a second opinion. One hospital, one file, one care team.

Today, Vitalis is a fully integrated healthcare campus offering 12 specialities, 60+ consultants, 24/7 emergency and critical care, advanced diagnostic imaging, a state-of-the-art cath lab, and a dedicated intensive care unit. Our NABH accreditation reflects our unwavering commitment to quality and patient safety.

Our promise

Clear pricing before treatment. A consultant who explains results in person, not just a report. And an emergency department that never asks you to wait to be seen.

We believe in patient-first philosophy: every decision is made with your well-being at the centre. Our research and teaching programmes ensure that we stay at the forefront of medical innovation, while our integrated care model means that your journey across departments is seamless, coordinated, and compassionate.
